Settings

Auto-lock

Lock timing and staying unlocked

Keeps the vault open through a refresh until the auto-lock time passes or you tap Lock. Turn this off on a shared computer.

Quick unlock

PIN and Face ID or Touch ID

Open the vault on this device with a 6-digit PIN or with Face ID or Touch ID, instead of typing the master password each time. Three wrong PINs in a row turn the PIN off, and the master password is needed again.

Two-factor sign-in

A code from your authenticator at sign-in

With this on, signing in on a new device needs your master password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (Apple Passwords, Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator and others). Devices you already use are not asked again. If you lose the authenticator, the recovery key gets you back in.

Where your account is signed in

Access log

Sign-ins and attempts on your account

Every sign-in, failed attempt and security change on your account, from any device. The server keeps the latest 500 events.

Version history

Go back to an earlier copy of your vault

The server keeps earlier encrypted copies of your vault: the last 5 saves, then older ones about an hour apart, up to 35. Going back never destroys anything. Entries added since then move to Recently deleted, and the copy you leave is kept here too.

Import passwords

From Google, Safari, Firefox and others

Bring in a CSV export from Google or Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass and similar. Entries already in the vault are skipped.

An export file holds every password in plain text. Delete it from your device and empty the trash as soon as the import is done.
